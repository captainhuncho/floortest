# 01 · Product requirements

FloorTest is a private receiving desk for unreleased music. A receiver (DJ, curator, label) shares one link; artists (senders) upload original files to it; the receiver listens, decides and downloads what they'll play; the sender sees what happened to their track.

Keep this file true to what is live. Any change to a feature updates its row here in the same commit (see `CLAUDE.md` → Docs).

## People

| Who | Account? | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Receiver | Yes (invite-only in the beta) | Owns a link `floortest.app/<handle>`, reviews, keeps, downloads |
| Sender | No (email code), or sends from their own account | Uploads tracks to a receiver's link, follows a receipt |
| Colleague | Yes | Listens to tracks a receiver shared (held: switch `sharing` off) |
| Ops team | Separate sign-in at ops.floortest.app | Super admin + Customer Care, Developer, Product Lead, Sales & Marketing (see 05) |

## Features (sender)

| Area | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Send to a link | One step at a time: the page asks only for the file; once it's chosen the form appears (title and artist read from the file; you; about the track: genre, mix and release status, AI use; DJ-sets permission). A message and artwork are behind "Add…" links until wanted. Problems are shown under the field they belong to, one at a time, saying what to do (no browser bubbles). Upload progress replaces the form in place (one bar, time left, Cancel); done shows a check, the receipt email, and Delivered → Listened → Replied. WAV/MP3 ≤ 250 MB, up to 3 tracks per send; email code first (no account). Name, email and profiles are remembered on the device after the first send. **Closed, full or paused link:** the page says why and offers "Email me when it reopens" (one email, sent when the link takes uploads again) | `/<handle>` |
| Folders (Pro/Team receivers) | EP/album up to 12 tracks within the receiver's folder size | `/<handle>` → Send folders |
| Review Together (Team receivers) | Up to 10 FloorTest accounts per collection (invite only) listen to tracks sent after they joined, vote keep/pass with a note, **comment**, keep **one shared shortlist**, and, if the owner turns on "Teammates can Keep / Not for me" (off by default), make the call. Never upload, reply or download; new music only via the owner's link. Owner's review panel shows the team's comments and shortlist (★ Shortlisted tag) | `/dashboard/collections/[id]` (Teammates), `/dashboard/shared/[id]` |
| Collection share links (held: switch `sharing` off) | On a collection page, **Share links**: New share link (name, Listen or Listen + Download, 7/14/30/90 days or until revoked, "people not on the list can ask to join"); the full address is shown once. Add people by email, `@company.com` (personal mail domains refused) or `@username`; remove anyone at once. Reset (new address, everyone signed out), Revoke, turn requests on/off; waiting requests with Approve (Listen or Listen + Download, how long) / Decline / Block; recent activity. Only tracks sent while the callout allowed sharing appear. The recipient opens `/c/<id>`: email code (or signed in with a confirmed email), accept the access notice, then listen (download if allowed) for up to 12 hours before verifying again; not on the list → ask to join (2 a day per email). Never indexed | `/dashboard/collections/[id]`, `/c/<id>` |
| Track artwork (Team receivers) | Optional JPG/PNG/WebP ≤ 25 MB, kept at original quality; one cover per folder | send form |
| Receipt | Follow one track. The headline is the strongest thing that has happened ("Maxwell listened." with the time; "Waiting for Maxwell to listen." until then), then the track, then "For you" (only when there is something to do: share consent, stems, confirm email), then "What's happened": a timeline where listens, downloads, replies and play-outs are bright and delivery, sharing and removal are quiet, with the steps still to come dimmed. "What these mean" and "Details" (email, privacy, retention, history link) fold away. Refreshes itself; a new status settles in once. `/sent` lists each send status-first | `/receipt/<token>`, `/sent` |

## Rules that define the product
- Uploads are originals, never transcoded. Previews (smaller stream copies) are planned for launch, made on the server only (see 06).
- Retention: undecided audio deleted after 90 days, "not for me" after 30 days; kept stays until the receiver deletes. Artwork is deleted with its track.
- Nothing is shared or released without the sender's permission; DJ-set use only if the sender ticks it.
- Sign-up and password reset never reveal whether an email has an account.
- Dates read one way everywhere: "29 Sept 2026" for days, "10 Oct, 17:04" for moments.

## Out of scope / held
Colleague sharing (built, switch off until after beta), stems (built, paid, hidden), card payments (Paddle, before paid launch), mobile app (parked), Rekordbox how-to video (parked).
