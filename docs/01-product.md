# 01 · Product requirements

FloorTest is a private receiving desk for new music: exclusives and works in progress that DJs test on the floor, and released tracks too. A receiver (DJ, curator, label) shares one link; artists (senders) upload original files to it; the receiver listens, decides and downloads what they'll play; the sender sees what happened to their track.

Keep this file true to what is live. Any change to a feature updates its row here in the same commit (see `CLAUDE.md` → Docs).

## People

| Who | Account? | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Receiver | Yes (invite-only in the beta) | Owns a link `floortest.app/<handle>`, reviews, keeps, downloads |
| Sender | No (email code), or sends from their own account | Uploads tracks to a receiver's link, follows a receipt |
| Share-link recipient | No (email code) or yes | Listens to a collection a receiver shared with them by name (held: switch `sharing` off) |
| Team member | Yes, their own login | Works on an organisation's desk (label, radio, crew) as A&R / Admin or Member; see Account teams |
| Ops team | Separate sign-in at ops.floortest.app. Metrics leads with the north star: tracks decided within 7 days of arriving, last 8 full weeks, by week, plan and solo/team desk (`src/lib/north-star.ts`) | Super admin + Customer Care, Developer, Product Lead, Sales & Marketing (see 05) |

- **Pull to refresh (owner's rule, 31 Oct):** every page (the desk, the public pages, sign in and sign up, message pages, ops) refreshes with a deliberate pull from the top. Phone: rest at the top for half a second, drag down (it's heavy: about a quarter screen of travel) and let go; pulling back up before letting go, swiping sideways, or holding still never refreshes. Mouse: press on empty page (not a control, words or a field), drag down, let go. Trackpad: pulling past the top only arms it; a "Refresh" button drops in for 4 seconds and only a click refreshes. A refresh reloads the page's data in place (what's playing and anything typed stay) and tells live lists like the Floor and the Review Queue to catch up. It never starts inside dialogs, sheets, the ⌘K panel, fields, the player, the batch bar, waveform scrubbing or the Review card swipe, and stays off on the sign-up password step and while a field holds typed, unsent words. Inside the iPhone app the app's own native pull to refresh does the job instead. Code: `src/lib/pull-refresh.ts` (the rules, unit-tested), `src/components/PullToRefresh.tsx` (mounted once in the root layout).
Left out of the mocks because the app has no data for them yet: Activity's "This week" and "Arrived today" tiles, Prepare's crate size and musical key, a contact's streaming links, Account's passkey count and sign-in alerts.

## Rules that define the product
- Uploads are originals, never transcoded. Previews (smaller stream copies) are planned for launch, made on the server only (see 06).
- Retention: undecided audio deleted after 90 days, "not for me" after 30 days; kept stays until the receiver deletes. Artwork is deleted with its track.
- Nothing is shared or released without the sender's permission; DJ-set use only if the sender ticks it.
- **Account types** (14 Oct): DJ, Producer / DJ, A&R, Label / Radio, Artist, Curator, Music professional. A type switches on the tools that desk is likely to need and never locks anything: Settings → Profile → Tools turns any tool on or off. The type belongs to the account, so every seat on a team desk sees the same tools; only the owner changes it. Price and limits never depend on type. Tools so far: **Played out** (the Monday question, the Tuesday email, the pick page and Played it out on Prepare), on by default for DJ, Producer / DJ and Curator. New type-specific features add a tool in `src/lib/account-types.ts` and check it with `hasTool()`.
- Sign-up and password reset never reveal whether an email has an account.
- Consent before tracking (owner, 20 Oct): a cookie banner asks first; visit counts (Vercel Analytics) and browser crash reports (Sentry) run only after Accept. Not now is as easy as Accept, both are remembered, and Cookie settings (footer, sign-in pages) asks again. On the ops host the banner's Cookie Policy link goes to the main site's /cookies (the ops host has no public pages).
- **Email-link and error pages** (owner, 31 Oct): every page opened from an email (share gate `/c/…`, team invite `/join/…`, `/verify-email/…`, `/confirm-email/…`, `/newsletter/confirm|unsubscribe/…`, `/unsubscribe/…`, `/sent/reopen/…`) and every error or not-found screen is one message: the logo on top, a small state mark, the title, one or two lines and one lime action, centred about 43% down; on the page on phones, on a card from 640px; light or dark with the device (`MessagePage`, `src/app/_ui/`).
