# 01 · Product requirements

FloorTest is a private receiving desk for unreleased music. A receiver (DJ, curator, label) shares one link; artists (senders) upload original files to it; the receiver listens, decides and downloads what they'll play; the sender sees what happened to their track.

Keep this file true to what is live. Any change to a feature updates its row here in the same commit (see `CLAUDE.md` → Docs).

## People

| Who | Account? | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Receiver | Yes (invite-only in the beta) | Owns a link `floortest.app/<handle>`, reviews, keeps, downloads |
| Sender | No (email code), or sends from their own account | Uploads tracks to a receiver's link, follows a receipt |
| Share-link recipient | No (email code) or yes | Listens to a collection a receiver shared with them by name (held: switch `sharing` off) |
| Team member | Yes, their own login | Works on an organisation's desk (label, radio, crew) as A&R / Admin or Member; see Account teams |
| Ops team | Separate sign-in at ops.floortest.app | Super admin + Customer Care, Developer, Product Lead, Sales & Marketing (see 05) |

## Features (sender)

| Area | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Send to a link | One step at a time: the page asks only for the file; once it's chosen the form appears (title and artist read from the file, with the track's BPM shown against the range the receiver asked for; you; about the track: genre, mix and release status, AI use as tap choices; DJ-sets permission). **The brief** shows only what the receiver asked for: their own genres (never the default list), BPM, wanted, deadline and note; tapping a genre there fills the form's Genre. A receiver's own genre list (6 at most) is tap choices in the form too, the long default list a dropdown. A message and artwork are behind "Add…" links until wanted. **What happens to the file** (who hears it, deletion, receipt) shows under the drop box and again above Send. Pressing Send with answers missing marks each field and lists them all above Send ("3 things left: genre, …", each one a link); no browser bubbles. On phones Send stays pinned at the bottom while the form scrolls. Upload progress replaces the form in place (one bar, time left, Cancel); done shows a check, the receipt email, Delivered → Listened → Downloaded → Replied, a receipt link per track (several tracks), and a note if optional artwork didn't upload. On desktop the page is two columns: the receiver and what happens to the file on the left, the brief and the form on the right. WAV/MP3 ≤ 250 MB, up to 3 tracks per send; email code first (no account). Name, email and profiles are remembered on the device after the first send, and then show as one "Sending as … Change" line. **Closed, full or paused link:** the page says why and offers "Email me when it reopens" (one email, sent when the link takes uploads again) | `/<handle>` |
| Folders (Pro/Team receivers) | EP/album up to 12 tracks within the receiver's folder size. Mix, release status and AI use are the same tap choices as single tracks. **Genre:** the sender says whether one genre covers every track or each track has its own (then every track needs one, starting from the folder's genre); a genre tapped in the brief starts as the folder's | `/<handle>` → Send folders |
| Review Together (part of Account teams) | On a desk with a team, every track's review panel has **Review together**: **comments** signed by the person (members delete their own; owners and admins any) and **one shared shortlist** (★ Shortlisted by …). Internal to the team; senders only see "heard by <account>'s team" on the send page. The 11 Oct per-collection guest list (Teammates box, guest votes, "Shared with you" for guests, "teammates can Keep / Not for me") is retired; roles decide who can make the call | review panel |
| Collection share links (held: switch `sharing` off) | On a collection page, **Share links**: New share link (name, Listen or Listen + Download, 7/14/30/90 days or until revoked, "people not on the list can ask to join"); the full address is shown once. Add people by email, `@company.com` (personal mail domains refused) or `@username`; remove anyone at once. Each person shows what they've done (not opened yet, or visits · played · downloaded · last seen; a company domain lists who came in through it); Remove asks first and ends their access at once. Reset (new address, everyone signed out), Revoke, turn requests on/off; waiting requests with Approve (Listen or Listen + Download, how long) / Decline / Block; recent activity. **Bell**: each person's first visit, downloads, and warnings (requests paused at 20 waiting; 10+ unknown addresses asking for codes in an hour, also emailed, at most once a day per link). **Accounts added by @username** are emailed and find it under Collections → **Shared with you to listen**, opening `/c/a.<number>` while signed in (works after a reset; signed out it asks them to log in and returns them there); visits opened this way end when they sign out. Only tracks sent while the callout allowed sharing **and under the private-sharing wording** appear (stored per track; tracks from the 1 Oct colleague model never do). The send form says, when the collection allows it: the receiver can share it privately with people they name, who confirm their email; listen, or listen and download; nobody can publish or release it. The sender can **Stop private sharing** from their receipt (asks first; the track leaves every link at once; one-way). The recipient page links to Terms and Privacy and its notice says the owner sees what they open, play and download. The recipient opens `/c/<id>`: email code (or signed in with a confirmed email), accept the access notice, then listen (download if allowed) for up to 12 hours before verifying again; not on the list → ask to join (2 a day per email). Never indexed | `/dashboard/collections/[id]`, `/c/<id>` |
| Account teams (switch `teams`, on since 11 Oct) | A label, radio station or crew has one account (the **Owner**'s) and invites the people who run it, each with their own FloorTest login. **Seats** by plan: Free 1, Pro 2 (you + 1), Team 5 (you + 4), plus add-on seats (set in ops for now). Seats get everything the plan includes, **only on that desk**: on their own account they're on their own plan. After a downgrade, the latest people to join past the seats are **paused** (shown on the Team page; they can't open the desk) until the owner removes someone or adds seats. **Desk bar** at the top: which desk you're on and as what, with **Switch desk**. **Roles:** Owner: everything. **A&R / Admin** (shown with that name; needs two-step sign-in, else works as a member): everything except plan, billing, account security, referrals and deleting the account; invites members; can change anyone's Keep / Not for me. Member: review, Keep / Not for me, internal notes, replies; can't change a call an Owner or A&R / Admin made (Undo brings a call back unlocked), delete, send as the account, pause the link, change where it sends, block senders or clear kept (none of these are shown to them; a refused action goes to the Team page, never an error screen), or open settings beyond Team. **Settings → Team**: people and seats, invite by email (7-day link; accepting needs that email; new people create their own login from it, even during the invite-only beta), remove, leave, roles (owner only), and **Replies from members**: straight away, or held for an approver the owner/admin picks (owners and admins can always approve); held replies can be edited, sent or not sent, and the approver's bell says how many wait. Each person on a team has their own bell (opening or clearing it doesn't touch anyone else's). Activity records who did each thing; senders only ever see the account's name | `/dashboard/settings/team`, `/join/<invite>` |
| Track artwork (Team receivers) | Optional JPG/PNG/WebP ≤ 25 MB, kept at original quality; one cover per folder | send form |
| Receipt | Follow one track. The headline is the strongest thing that has happened ("Maxwell listened." with the time; "Waiting for Maxwell to listen." until then), then the track, then "For you" (only when there is something to do: stems, confirm email), then "What's happened": a timeline where listens, downloads, replies and play-outs are bright and delivery is quiet, with the steps still to come dimmed. The receipt never shows when a file left the desk ("Not for me" files leave sooner than undecided ones, so the date would give the decision away); a track not listened to within 90 days of delivery says "Not listened to in 90 days." The stems request and **Stop private sharing** stay as they were after the file leaves too: stems sent then are checked, listed as sent, and thrown away; the switch still answers "stopped". "What these mean" and "Details" (email, privacy, retention, history link) fold away. Refreshes itself; a new status settles in once. `/sent` lists each send status-first | `/receipt/<token>`, `/sent` |

## Rules that define the product
- Uploads are originals, never transcoded. Previews (smaller stream copies) are planned for launch, made on the server only (see 06).
- Retention: undecided audio deleted after 90 days, "not for me" after 30 days; kept stays until the receiver deletes. Artwork is deleted with its track.
- Nothing is shared or released without the sender's permission; DJ-set use only if the sender ticks it.
- Sign-up and password reset never reveal whether an email has an account.
- Dates read one way everywhere: "29 Sept 2026" for days, "10 Oct, 17:04" for moments.
