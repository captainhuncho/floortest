# Sender features

The features listed under Features (sender) in [01 · Product requirements](../01-product.md), one section each. Keep each section true to what is live and update it in the same commit as the feature.

## Send to a link

One step at a time: the page asks only for the file; once it's chosen the form appears (title and artist read from the file, with the track's BPM shown against the range the receiver asked for; you; about the track: genre, mix and release status, AI use as tap choices; DJ-sets permission). **The brief** shows only what the receiver asked for: their own genres (never the default list), BPM, wanted, deadline and note; tapping a genre there fills the form's Genre. A receiver's own genre list (6 at most) is tap choices in the form too, the long default list a dropdown (Afro House, Amapiano, Gqom, Afrobeats, House and the rest: `DEFAULT_GENRES` in src/lib/plans.ts; Gqom added 1 Nov, owner). A message and artwork are behind "Add…" links until wanted. **What happens to the file** (who hears it, deletion, receipt) shows under the drop box and again above Send. Pressing Send with answers missing marks each field and lists them all above Send ("3 things left: genre, …", each one a link); no browser bubbles. On phones Send stays pinned at the bottom while the form scrolls. Upload progress replaces the form in place (one bar, time left, Cancel); done shows a check, the receipt email, Delivered → Listened → Downloaded → Replied, a receipt link per track (several tracks), and a note if optional artwork didn't upload. **One send, one alert (owner, 29 Oct):** several tracks sent together (up to 3 from the link, or a folder) make one "Delivered" email to the sender listing every track with its own receipt link, one "New tracks" email to the receiver (instant mode) and one bell row ("3 new tracks from Ade"); a single track keeps its own wording. The email goes once the send's last track is saved; if one never arrives (refused, cancelled), the rest go 15 minutes after the latest one, never listing the failed one (`src/lib/send-receipts.ts`). One column at every width (owner, 29 Oct): the receiver's photo, "Send music to", their name and "Receiving for" the callout centred at the top and kept there once a file is picked; below a line, the brief, the drop box and what happens to the file, left-aligned; once a file is picked the form takes the brief's place. Once the profile scrolls out of view, a slim bar comes down from the top with the photo, name and callout, and leaves again when the profile is back. The page follows the device's light or dark mode (owner, 29 Oct), with the browser bar to match. WAV/MP3 ≤ 250 MB, up to 3 tracks per send; email code first (no account). Name, email and profiles are remembered on the device after the first send, and then show as one "Sending as … Change" line. **Closed, full or paused link:** the page says why and offers "Email me when it reopens" (one email, sent when the link takes uploads again)

Where: `/<handle>`

## Terms tick (sender without an account)

On a sender's first send, and again whenever the Terms text changes, the send form (the link page and Send folders) shows one tick: "I have read, understood and agree to the Terms, including that music I send may be played by the receiver, and have read the Privacy Policy.", Terms and Privacy Policy linked. After a change it also says "The Terms were updated {date}. Read the whole Terms". Send stays off until it's ticked ("Tick the box above to send."). The server refuses the send unless the tick is for the current text (the form sends the text's fingerprint); ticking the same text twice adds no record. The sender's first Delivered email per Terms version carries the stamped PDF copy. Senders with an account accepted at sign-up and see no tick.

Where: `/<handle>`, Send folders, `src/app/[handle]/TermsTick.tsx`, `src/lib/terms-record.ts`

## Send rules by receiver type (owner, 15 Nov)

The per-send "Can they play it in DJ sets?" switch is gone. Sending to a label or A&R desk (`kind` `label` or `ar`, legacy `label_radio`; `receiverAsksPlayChoice`) shows "Sending to a label" with Evaluation only (default) or OK to play, and a line saying what the pick means; a folder gets one choice for every track. Every other desk is DJ-style: no choice, and the Terms tick covers play-out. The form sends `play` only to label desks; the server ignores the old `sets_ok` field. The note above Send says "They can play it out only if you pick OK to play above" on label desks and drops the sets sentence elsewhere.

The signed-in Send page (`/dashboard/send`) asks the same: the receiver lookup carries `asksPlay`. Tested end to end in `e2e/label-send.spec.ts` (e2e-only label desk `lunalabel`).

Where: `/<handle>`, `/dashboard/send`, Send folders, `src/app/[handle]/Choice.tsx` (`PLAY_CHOICES`), `src/lib/account-types.ts`, `src/lib/receiver-lookup.ts`

## Folders (Pro/Team receivers)

EP/album up to 12 tracks within the receiver's folder size. Mix, release status and AI use are the same tap choices as single tracks. **Genre:** the sender says whether one genre covers every track or each track has its own (then every track needs one, starting from the folder's genre); a genre tapped in the brief starts as the folder's

Where: `/<handle>` → Send folders

## Review Together (part of Account teams)

On a desk with a team, every track's review panel has **Review together**: **comments** signed by the person (members delete their own; owners and admins any) and **one shared shortlist** (★ Shortlisted by …). Internal to the team; senders only see "heard by <account>'s team" on the send page. The 11 Oct per-collection guest list (Teammates box, guest votes, "Shared with you" for guests, "teammates can Keep / Not for me") is retired; roles decide who can make the call

Where: review panel

## Collection share links (held: switch `sharing` off)

On a collection page, **Share links**: New share link (name, Listen or Listen + Download, 7/14/30/90 days or until revoked, "people not on the list can ask to join"); the full address is shown once. Add people by email, `@company.com` (personal mail domains refused) or `@username`; remove anyone at once. Each person shows what they've done (not opened yet, or visits · played · downloaded · last seen; a company domain lists who came in through it); Remove asks first and ends their access at once. Reset (new address, everyone signed out), Revoke, turn requests on/off; waiting requests with Approve (Listen or Listen + Download, how long) / Decline / Block; recent activity. **Bell**: each person's first visit, downloads, and warnings (requests paused at 20 waiting; 10+ unknown addresses asking for codes in an hour, also emailed, at most once a day per link). **Accounts added by @username** are emailed and find it under Collections → **Shared with you to listen**, opening `/c/a.<number>` while signed in (works after a reset; signed out it asks them to log in and returns them there); visits opened this way end when they sign out. Only tracks sent while the callout allowed sharing **and under the private-sharing wording** appear (stored per track; tracks from the 1 Oct colleague model never do). The send form says, when the collection allows it: the receiver can share it privately with people they name, who confirm their email; listen, or listen and download; nobody can publish or release it. The sender can **Stop private sharing** from their receipt (asks first; the track leaves every link at once; one-way). The recipient page links to Terms and Privacy and its notice says the owner sees what they open, play and download. The recipient opens `/c/<id>`: email code (or signed in with a confirmed email), accept the access notice, then listen (download if allowed) for up to 12 hours before verifying again; not on the list → ask to join (2 a day per email). Never indexed

Where: `/dashboard/collections/[id]`, `/c/<id>`

## Callout share card

While your link sends to an open callout (account owner only), the link's **Share** button on Floor, Account, Collections and the collection page opens a sheet with a Story or Square image of that callout. Share image (phones), Save image, Copy caption, Copy link, Share link only. With no open callout, or for team members, Share shares the plain link as before. Cards work while the site is behind the Coming soon password, since receiving links open without it (owner, 11 Nov). No third-party script.

## Account teams (switch `teams`: on 11–15 Oct, off on the live site since the 15 Oct beta scope; teams work only on ops super-admin desks)

Owners manage the team (invites, roles, removal, reply approval) only with two-step sign-in on; the Team page links to Security until then. **Beta (owner, 15 Oct):** switch `teams` off for everyone; teams still work on a desk owned by an ops super admin, for the owner's own testing (`teamsOn(deskId)`). A label, radio station or crew has one account (the **Owner**'s) and invites the people who run it, each with their own FloorTest login. **Seats** by plan: Free 1, Pro 2 (you + 1), Team 5 (you + 4), plus add-on seats (set in ops for now). Seats get everything the plan includes, **only on that desk**: on their own account they're on their own plan. After a downgrade, the latest people to join past the seats are **paused** (shown on the Team page; they can't open the desk) until the owner removes someone or adds seats. **Desk bar** at the top: which desk you're on and as what, with **Switch desk**. **Roles:** Owner: everything. **A&R / Admin** (shown with that name; needs two-step sign-in, else works as a member): everything except plan, billing, account security, referrals and deleting the account; invites members; can change anyone's Keep / Not for me. Member: review, Keep / Not for me, internal notes, replies; can't change a call an Owner or A&R / Admin made, even one made while the member had the track open (checked when the change is saved; Undo brings a call back unlocked, and only while the track still holds the call being undone), delete, send as the account, pause the link, change where it sends, block senders or clear kept (none of these are shown to them; a refused action goes to the Team page, never an error screen), or open settings beyond Team. **Settings → Team**: people and seats, invite by email (7-day link; accepting needs that email; new people create their own login from it, even during the invite-only beta), remove, leave, roles (owner only; a refused role change or invite cancel says why and isn't logged as done), and **Replies from members**: straight away, or held for an approver the owner/admin picks (owners and admins can always approve); held replies can be edited, sent or not sent, and the approver's bell says how many wait. Each person on a team has their own bell (opening or clearing it doesn't touch anyone else's). Activity records who did each thing and shows the teammate's name beside it (a Member an owner or A&R / Admin ticked for callouts can create callouts and set their close dates and caps, owner 16 Nov; choosing the collection that receives stays with the owner and A&R / Admins; Members mark played out without approval: everyone answers for their own actions); teammates see the desk's log, never the holder's sign-ins, security, plan or alert settings; Undo only takes back your own Keep / Not for me; senders only ever see the account's name

Where: `/dashboard/settings/team`, `/join/<invite>`

## Receipt

Follow one track. The headline is the strongest thing that has happened ("Maxwell listened." with the time; "Waiting for Maxwell to listen." until then), then the track, then "For you" (only when there is something to do: stems, confirm email), then "What's happened": a timeline where every step that has happened has a filled dot and the newest is lime, earlier ones bone: until the receiver does something, Delivered is lit ("to Maxwell's desk for Afterlife"), so the sender sees the track arrived; listens, downloads, replies and play-outs are bold; steps still to come are hollow and dimmed. A track unheard for 90 days lights nothing. The receipt never shows when a file left the desk ("Not for me" files leave sooner than undecided ones, so the date would give the decision away); a track not listened to within 90 days of delivery says "Not listened to in 90 days." The stems request and **Stop private sharing** stay as they were after the file leaves too: stems sent then are checked, listed as sent, and thrown away; the switch still answers "stopped". "What these mean" and "Details" (email, privacy, retention, history link) fold away. The sender's email shows masked (`m•••@g•••.com`, owner 2 Nov), since anyone holding the link sees the page. **Details need proof (owner, 2 Nov, Option B):** with the link alone the receipt shows only what happened and when (Delivered, Listened, Downloaded, Replied, Played out, with their times; "The receiver listened."), and the sender's email masked. The track, artist, receiver, callout, played-out note, waveform, stems, Stop private sharing and "Send another" show only to the sender, proven by: the browser the track was sent from (the `ft_receipt` cookie, set when the send completes), the FloorTest account it was sent from, signed in, or **Show my details**: a 6-digit code emailed to the sender's own address (never typed), then remembered in that browser for 30 days. Every receipt action and the stems upload check the proof on the server. The live status works without it. Once the site is public, senders without an account see one quiet line at the foot: "DJ too? Get your own link…" (sign-up, or the waitlist while it's invite-only). Refreshes itself; a new status settles in once. `/sent` lists each send status-first, lime from delivery on (dim only after 90 days unheard), as do the Send page's sent rows. From the track's final outcome the receipt is read-only ("This receipt is read-only now… The link closes on …": no stem upload, no stop sharing), and 90 days later its link shows only "This receipt has closed" with Email me my sends; the same clock for every track: read-only once played out or on day 90, whichever is first, and an open stems request keeps it open until a stem arrives, so the decision never shows (owner, 4 Nov)

Where: `/receipt/<token>`, `/sent`
