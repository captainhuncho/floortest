# Ops features

The features listed under Features (ops) in [01 · Product requirements](../01-product.md), one section each. Keep each section true to what is live and update it in the same commit as the feature.

## Edit the site on the page

Ops staff with Site content open the real page from ops (**Edit on the page ↗**): click text to type, click a list (steps, questions) to edit it, click the product image to replace it, drag sections (or ↑ ↓) and hide/show them (the bar above each section is always there in edit mode, on phones too, no hover), and set any text or list Smaller, Default, Larger or Largest (owner, 29 Oct; relative to its designed size, so the type scale holds on every screen; headlines stop growing at 15vw so nothing overflows a 320px phone). Saves to a draft; **Publish** puts it live; versions kept to restore. Home has all of this, and since 29 Oct every visible line of it is editable (since home 1E, 20 Nov: the hero and its warehouse messages, each day of the week story from Sunday to the morning after, the link card's two rows, the review card's replies, the receipt's steps, the three "fair to the people sending" points, the end claim title, waitlist, header links, footer lines; the sections are week, booth, receipt, fair, plans, questions, end claim and waitlist, with privacy, journey, how and preview kept but hidden by default); Pricing, Sign-up/Log-in, the public link page and receipts have text editing and sizes (wording with fill-ins like {receiver} opens in a box). Not editable on purpose: sample data inside product pictures (track names, artists, BPM and lengths in the queue, review card, crate and tick list), button labels that change with sign-in state, form fields and consent lines, plan cards (Admin → Plans), and the legal pages

Where: ops → Site content, then floortest.app in edit mode

## Site lock (switch `site_lock`, on by default; 13 Nov)

The coming-soon gate's on/off, in Admin → Feature switches (super admin with two-step sign-in; every flip in the admin log). On: floortest.app shows only Coming soon until a visitor enters the site password, signs in or follows a live invite; receiving links, receipts, legal pages and Contact stay open. Off: the site is open to everyone, no password asked. Turning it on again re-locks every signed-out browser (old passes stop working); people signed in keep working (owner, 14 Nov). The password itself stays in `SITE_PASSWORD`; without it the site is open whatever the switch says. If the switch can't be read the site stays locked. Previews never lock. Turning it off on production is the owner's own step. **How the gate looks (owner, 17 Nov, option 55):** option 54's club photo and glass (`src/app/coming-soon/`, the scene and tokens shared from `src/app/(auth)/`). Desktop: the photo fills the page; FloorTest and Beta at the top left, "Already have an account? Log in" (to `/login`) at the top right; at the left the eyebrow "Invite-only beta", "Coming soon" as the one big line, the line about the password, the field "Have the password?", Enter (lime) and Join the waitlist (glass); dark glass at night, the words straight on the day photo by day. Phones: the photo in the top part and one frosted sheet at the bottom holding the same, which paints before the photo. A wrong password shows "That password isn't right." under the field (read out) and keeps what was typed. Join the waitlist swaps the form for the waitlist in the same panel (desktop) or as a plain full screen (phones; the photo comes back on "You're on the list"); same questions, bot check and answer. Reduce Motion stills the photo and the dot. **Launch order (owner, 14 Nov):** lock off first with **Open sign-up** still off, so the home page offers the waitlist and only personal invites open accounts; Open sign-up is turned on later, after the waitlist.

## Ops → Notices

A thin strip at the top of the signed-in dashboard for an upcoming short update ("Short update Sun 15 Nov, 02:00–02:20. Uploads pause for a few minutes."). Staff with Announcements write a title (up to 80 characters), one line of detail (up to 200) and an optional link to a FloorTest page, and pick four times: show from, update starts, update ends, hide after. Plain text only, no exclamation marks. One notice shows at a time, the soonest; times show in each person's own time zone. The page lists Showing now, Upcoming and Past, with Edit and End early (it stops showing at once). Each create, edit and end goes in the admin log. On the dashboard the strip sits under the top bar as a status (read out politely), with a 44px close button; closing is saved on the server for that person (their own row), so a closed notice is never rendered for them again, on any device, with no flash. Never shown in admin View as

Where: ops → Growth → Notices; every `/dashboard` page

Platform card (Incidents or Jobs permission only): health score and the checks that aren't OK (same checks as before), the latest open incident, job runs logged today and how many failed. ## Ops kit and ⌘K (admin redesign B, step 1, 1 Nov)

One set of controls for every ops screen, in `src/app/admin/kit/` (DESIGN.md "Ops kit"). **OpsButton:** a press answers at once, a spinner only after 150 ms, then ✓ in place or the reason with Retry; Feedback's status switch uses it. **Toasts:** up to three, pause on hover or focus, Undo and ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z when the result offers one, "Sending in 5 s · Undo" for held sends. **Held sends (1 Nov):** resending an email or an invite, inviting from the waitlist (one or the oldest N), personal invites by email, approving or declining a plan request, verifying someone (their request, a proposal, or directly), and rejecting or removing a verification queue their email 8 s ahead (this process sends it 0.5 s after the hold, so an early timer never leaves it to the mail job) (`outbox.next_at`, `sendMailHeld`); the action answers with a signed undo token (`src/lib/ops-undo.ts`, same admin, within the hold) and `undoSend` stops the emails never tried (one that failed once and is waiting to retry may have reached them, so it stays) and, in the same transaction, puts back what changed (plan and request reopened; waitlist entry waiting again and its unsent code revoked; a swapped invite code restored; a personal invite's code revoked; the Verified mark taken off or put back with the account's earlier status and note, the proposal waiting again or, if the action wrote it, closed as withdrawn), answering "Not sent" or "Too late: it was sent". On screen the result toast counts "Sending in 5 s" down with Undo and ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z, then says "Sent" and closes; hovering or focusing it pauses the count, but Undo always closes 1 s before the server's 8 s, and after Undo the page refreshes so the row is back (`src/app/admin/kit/Toaster.tsx`, `heldLeft` in `kit/core.ts`). The admin log gets "sent" once delivered and "cancelled" on Undo. If the server stops mid-hold the mail job sends it on its next run (every 10 min, so up to about 10 min late). Security and automatic emails are never held. **Confirm:** names who it affects (first 8, then "and N more"), asks a reason when needed, starts on Cancel. **⌘K / Ctrl+K:** people by name, @handle or email (only with Users (view): 2+ characters, at most 8, 60 searches a minute per staff member, emails only where the Users page shows them), the pages this person may open, and "Keyboard shortcuts"; Enter opens the record. **Keys:** one manager, off in fields and dialogs, keys pressed while an action runs wait their turn; "?" lists them. **Users selection:** Shift-click ranges, j/k, x, Shift+J/K, ⌘A, Esc; a floating glass bar tags or suspends (limits unchanged: 50 picked, 20 suspended)

Where: ops → every page (⌘K), Feedback, Users

## Growth & decisions

From FloorTest's own data: at the top, the beta numbers ("beta week X of 8"; beta accounts are every account, whenever it joined, except ADMIN_EMAILS, ops staff, `@floortest.test` and suspended accounts, split invited / other and joined before 1 Nov / since; activity counts from 1 Nov 00:00 UTC): weekly active receivers (kept or passed a track in the last 7 days, target 15), activated receivers (got a track since 1 Nov from someone other than the desk or its team, same rule as below, and decided on one, target 25), tracks per active receiver (tracks from real senders in the last 7 days counted from 1 Nov ÷ weekly active, "—" with none active, target 10+ a week) and median time to first listen (delivered to first play, target under 48 h; tracks sent from the account's own or a teammate's email left out, an inferred rule; with tracks listened and waiting); under 5 accounts in a base shows the raw number only (no share, target, bar or pace). Four tiles, 2×2 on phones, 4 across on wide screens. Under each count tile: "by today you'd expect about Y" on a straight line from 0 to the target at week 8, scaled by the days gone (round(target × days / 56), so day 1 expects 0), with ahead / on pace / behind, and, when there are fewer beta accounts than the target, how many more accounts it would take even if all counted; in the beta's first week a note says how many days the 7-day window really covers ("Counting since 1 Nov, so this week covers N days"), since Overview's 7 days reach back before 1 Nov. In the funnel (people who joined in the last 90 days, not the same group as the beta numbers), a step after one that reached nobody shows "—" instead of a share. While the beta runs (to the end of week 8) the findings lead with activation drops (signed up → got a track → kept a track) and put the paying drop last. Then findings with the counts behind them (biggest drop, best source and account type by share paying, sign-up pace, 4-week projection with a likely range), waitlist-to-paying funnel (90 days), sign-ups for 12 weeks plus a 4-week projection, sources and account types (people, got a track, pay; groups under 5 marked), "Do they stay?" weekly cohorts, and the sales pipeline from plan requests (monthly revenue for roles with Revenue). ## User account (ops)

One page per account: who they are, status chips, tags and notes, usage, senders, emails sent, messages, history and their activity, with actions on the side. **Verified mark box (owner, 1 Nov):** shows the state (Verified since a date with the note on how it was checked, Request waiting, Not verified) for everyone who can open the account. Every staff verification has a **reason and proof** (owner, 1 Nov): a reason (10–500 characters) and at least one screenshot or PDF (PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF, up to 5, 10 MB each, checked by their first bytes) or one https profile link. The super admin and people given **Verification (add the mark directly)** get **Verify profile…** (verified at once, emailed, kept as an approved proposal); everyone else with Verification gets **Propose verification…** → Send for approval, and can withdraw it while it waits. Refused for suspended accounts, unconfirmed emails, or when a proposal is already waiting. **Remove verification…** when it's verified (a reason, sent to them). Under a fold, **History**: every proposal with who, when, reason, links, proof and the decision

Where: ops → Users → an account

## Verification

**Proposals waiting** on top (owner, 1 Nov): each staff proposal with the account, proposer, reason, links and proof (image thumbnails, PDFs by name, opened through a permission-checked link); people given **Verification (approve or decline proposals)** Approve (adds the mark, emails them) or Decline… with a note; nobody approves their own; the proposer can Withdraw. Requests waiting, each as a three-step tracker with the bio code and the profiles to check (Approve for people who may verify directly or approve, recorded as an approved proposal; **Forward for approval** for everyone else, which opens the account's Propose form; Not yet, which only turns down a request that is still waiting, so a verified account keeps its mark and verified time); **Verify someone directly** (owner, 1 Nov): search by @handle or email (at most 10 results, each with its state) and open the account to verify it without a request; protected names; everyone verified, with Revoke. A direct verification sets the mark at once, keeps the note on the account and in the admin log ("verified identity (no request)"), and emails them. Both ways send one editable email, **Verified** (Site content → Emails): after a request it says they can remove the code from their bio, after a direct check that the team checked their profile and added it

Where: ops → Verification

## Moderation

Search every account by track, artist, sender or receipt token; results on the left, the picked track on the right (on phones, on its own with a way back): a waveform player, sender, receiver, received, file, the sender's history (tracks, receivers, blocks), the track's recent activity, and Keep / Block sender / Block everywhere / Remove track. **Keep** (1 Nov) marks the track reviewed and left up: "Kept by @x" with the date on the track, a Reviewed chip in the results, a line in the admin log; it loads no audio. **Listening (owner, 1 Nov; on hold except for disputes):** staff with Moderation can play a track only while it has an open takedown claim; any other track shows "Listening is only for tracks with an open takedown claim", and the audio link answers 404 (as for a missing track) and logs "refused play"; nothing loads until Play, the file comes from a 5-minute link, and each play start is written to the admin log (the same admin playing the same track again within 10 minutes is not logged twice). **The new Moderation (admin redesign B, step 4; behind the `ops_shell` switch, off: the page above as it was):** a three-pane signal queue that lists only tracks with a real signal (`src/lib/ops-signals.ts`): an open takedown claim, a sender some receiver blocked (their newest track), or an upload whose virus check is late (not a track yet: no player, no Keep, a link to Jobs). Left: "Tracks with a signal · N", newest first, filter tabs All / Takedown / Blocked / Scanner with counts, and a search box that filters the list as you type (title, artist, sender, receipt token) and on Enter runs today's search across every account (each result carries its signal, or "No signals" / "Reviewed by @x"). Each row: title, artist · @receiver, age and the signal in words ("Takedown claim by X", "Blocked by N receivers", "Scanner checked it late"); empty: "No signals right now". Middle: the track (title, artist, to @receiver, length, size), the signal banner, the player for takedown claims only (anything else: a still waveform and "Listening is only for tracks with an open takedown claim"), the sender's facts and the track's history. Right (a dialog below 1280 px; on phones a row opens a sheet): Keep (marks it reviewed by @you, "✓ Kept", then the next row opens; no undo, Keep has none), Remove (asks for a reason first, kept in the admin log), Block sender for @receiver (asks first), Block everywhere (asks first; Sender blocks permission only) and Skip. Keys: j/k move, a keep, x remove, b block sender, Space plays (takedown claims only; never while a button has focus), ? lists them; a key pressed while an action runs waits its turn. The rail and panel show the number of tracks with a signal beside Moderation and drop it after Keep or Remove. Code: `src/app/admin/moderation/queue/` (rules in `model.ts`, tested in `tests/ops-moderation.test.ts`; e2e `e2e/ops-moderation.spec.ts`)

Where: ops → Moderation
