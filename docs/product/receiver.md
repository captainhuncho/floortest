# Receiver features

The features listed under Features (receiver) in [01 · Product requirements](../01-product.md), one section each. Keep each section true to what is live and update it in the same commit as the feature.

## Sign-up / sign-in

One calm column at every width (no side panel). Invite-only during the beta (switch `open_signup` opens it): only a personal invite sent from Ops to that exact email opens an account, so there is no code to type. Each invite opens one account and lasts 14 days (Resend gives a fresh 14); a used or expired invite link shows "This invite has been used or has expired" with Log in, never the sign-up form (owner, 5 Nov); shared codes and referral codes don't open anything while it's off (`src/lib/invites.ts`). Ops → Invites lists pending and joined invites; revoked ones (cancelled, or old codes Resend replaced) move to a closed "Revoked (n)" list at the foot of the page (owner, 29 Oct). Each revoked one has **Restore** (works again; refused if that email already has an account or another invite waiting; an old short code still can't open the coming-soon page, so Resend it after) and **Delete for good** (not for a code someone signed up with: removes the invite and the invite emails that carry its code from the outbox in one go; Ops log lines that mention it stay, and a "deleted invite" line is added). Both ask first. Sign-up asks only for what the account needs, one question per screen (owner, 31 Oct): welcome, first name and surname, username (checked live), account type as tap rows (DJ, Producer / DJ, A&R, Label, Radio, Artist, Curator, Music professional; see Account types below), then email (locked to the invite's), password, the 18+/Terms tick and the two optional email ticks, and **Create my link** sends it all at once. Then check your email (tap the link; nobody waits for it), add a photo (or skip) and "Your link is ready" with Copy and Share, then the Floor. Back and a reload keep the answers (never the password). The stage or label name is set later in Profile (the username shows until then). Email + password, emailed one-time code, or Continue with Google (Gmail/Workspace only, Google account pinned, password to approve first link; website only, hidden inside the iPhone and Android apps for App Review 4.8, owner 15 Nov: the app adds `FloorTestApp` to its user agent, `src/lib/in-app.ts`; there log-in says "Signed up with Google? Use Email me a sign-in code", since the emailed code reaches any account; a missing or spoofed marker just shows Google again). Problems are shown under the field, one at a time; the server's answer sits by the button. Optional two-factor (authenticator + recovery codes; turning it on needs the password and signs out other devices, with an email), sign-in alerts. First visit lands on the Floor with "Welcome, {first}." and the link first

Where: `/signup`, `/login`, `/login/code`, `/signup/google`, `/login/google` **Bot protection (13 Nov):** a throwaway inbox is refused ("Use a permanent email address."); sign-up is capped per connection (10 an hour, 20 a day) and site-wide (100 an hour), much tighter when the bot check couldn't check; a new account's link page says "isn't open yet" and takes no uploads until its email is confirmed, and a link name left unconfirmed for 7 days is freed (SECURITY.md).

## Terms acceptance (15 Nov, Research brief)

The sign-up tick (guided and Google sign-up) reads "I'm 18 or over, and I have read, understood and agree to the Terms, including that music I send may be played by the receiver, and have read the Privacy Policy", with Terms and Privacy Policy linked. Ticking it records the Terms version, the time (UTC) and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the exact text, and emails a PDF copy stamped "Accepted by {name}, {email}, {date and time UTC}, Terms version {date}". When the Terms text changes, the next visit to the desk opens **Accept the Terms** ("The Terms have changed" if they accepted an older text): one tick, **Accept and continue**, then the Floor.

Nobody uses the desk or the phone app without agreeing to the current Terms (owner, 15 Nov): every desk page, action and API call, and every app request, waits for the tick; when the Terms can't be loaded, nobody gets in until they can. Someone who doesn't want to agree can still ask for a copy of their data or delete their account from the Terms page. Admins tick on their own desk; the ops console is not gated.

Where: `/signup`, `/signup/google`, `/accept-terms`, `src/lib/terms-record.ts`, `src/lib/auth.ts`, `src/lib/app-auth.ts` (`appUserAgreed`), `/api/v1/terms`

Share it and tracks land here.", the link row, full width under the greeting at every size, with where it sends ("Sends tracks to your Review Queue"), then Getting started; no crates, no zeros, no Your week row. On a desk that already has tracks, Getting started sits last. **Notices**, one at a time under the top bar, in this order: View as (the desk frame's strip), confirm your email (the banner, on the Floor and Send only), then one strip for the link ("Your link is paused until {date}." with Open Account, where the switch lives; "{Collection} closed on {date}. Nobody can send you music right now." with "Send new tracks to {open collection}" or New collection; a full desk), then "Your desk is nearly full: N of N tracks. Download your kept tracks, then clear them." with Prepare, from 80% of the plan's limit. Buttons that change the desk are left out for Members and in View as. **On a teammate's desk** the greeting uses the person's own first name, the line reads "N tracks came in on Max's desk this week." and the row reads "Max's link". **While it loads** a skeleton in the same layout shows (no pulse with Reduce Motion); **if it fails**: "Your Floor didn't load. ## Collections

**Folders with covers (owner, 17 Nov: option 43, "Good to go"; this replaces the cards, the Callouts / Projects switch and the collection page described below where they differ).** Collections is one list of folders: the folders the desk made itself first, then every callout and every project, each with a cover, its name and one line ("Receiving · Callout · 12 tracks", "Project · 8 tracks", "Folder · 3 callouts"); the receiving callout first, then open callouts and projects (newest first), then closed callouts. **Covers show the music, never a folder icon (owner, 17 Nov: option 57 I, "four covers"; `FolderCover` in `collections/FolderRow.tsx`, art picked in `src/lib/covers.ts`).** A callout's art is its own artwork first, then its newest tracks that have artwork (senders' attached artwork, newest first), four at most; a folder the desk made uses the cover of each callout inside, newest first. With four pieces of art the cover is a 2x2 square (no gaps, top left first, clipped to the rounded corner: 10px on a 56px phone row, 14px in the grid and on the page); with one to three it shows the first, full size, never repeated to fill four. No art at all: the plain tile (`well`, `line` border) with a small music note in `faint`. Nothing inside (a callout with no tracks and no artwork, an empty project or folder): a dashed square. Projects always show the plain tile or the dashed square: project tracks (the desk's own uploads) carry no artwork yet (project artwork: later; DESIGN accepted this, 17 Nov). Soft shadow only, no glow, no lime. Rows and tiles ask for the small copy (`?size=thumb` on `/api/track-art/<id>` and `/api/collection-art/<id>`: a 192px WebP, made on request after the same access check, kept by the browser no longer than access lasts, nothing extra stored; `src/lib/art-thumb.ts`); a folder's own page shows the full artwork. Images below the fold load lazily. The cover is decoration (`alt=""`, hidden from screen readers); the row's link still says what it is. Phones show rows with a search field ("Search your folders", narrows by name in the browser) and an **All / Callouts / Projects** filter (a tab list; Projects keeps its address, `/dashboard/projects`, where the tracks in no project also live); from tablet width the folders are a grid of covers (each at least 180px wide, so names fit). - **Pull to refresh (owner's rule, 31 Oct):** every page (the desk, the public pages, sign in and sign up, message pages, ops) refreshes with a deliberate pull from the top. Phone: rest at the top for half a second, drag down (it's heavy: about a quarter screen of travel) and let go; pulling back up before letting go, swiping sideways, or holding still never refreshes. Mouse: press on empty page (not a control, words or a field), drag down, let go. Trackpad: pulling past the top only arms it; a "Refresh" button drops in for 4 seconds and only a click refreshes. A refresh reloads the page's data in place (what's playing and anything typed stay) and tells live lists like the Floor and the Review Queue to catch up. It never starts inside dialogs, sheets, the ⌘K panel, fields, the player, the batch bar, waveform scrubbing or the Review card swipe, and stays off on the sign-up password step and while a field holds typed, unsent words. Inside the iPhone app the app's own native pull to refresh does the job instead.
