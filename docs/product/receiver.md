# Receiver features

The features listed under Features (receiver) in [01 · Product requirements](../01-product.md), one section each. Keep each section true to what is live and update it in the same commit as the feature.

## Sign-up / sign-in

One calm column at every width (no side panel). Invite-only during the beta (switch `open_signup` opens it): only a personal invite sent from Ops to that exact email opens an account, so there is no code to type. Each invite opens one account and lasts 14 days (Resend gives a fresh 14); a used or expired invite link shows "This invite has been used or has expired" with Log in, never the sign-up form (owner, 5 Nov); shared codes and referral codes don't open anything while it's off (`src/lib/invites.ts`). Ops → Invites lists pending and joined invites; revoked ones (cancelled, or old codes Resend replaced) move to a closed "Revoked (n)" list at the foot of the page (owner, 29 Oct). Each revoked one has **Restore** (works again; refused if that email already has an account or another invite waiting; an old short code still can't open the coming-soon page, so Resend it after) and **Delete for good** (not for a code someone signed up with: removes the invite and the invite emails that carry its code from the outbox in one go; Ops log lines that mention it stay, and a "deleted invite" line is added). Both ask first. Sign-up asks only for what the account needs, one question per screen (owner, 31 Oct): welcome, first name and surname, username (checked live), account type as tap rows (DJ, Producer / DJ, A&R, Label, Radio, Artist, Curator, Music professional; see Account types below), then email (locked to the invite's), password, the 18+/Terms tick and the two optional email ticks, and **Create my link** sends it all at once. Then check your email (tap the link; nobody waits for it), add a photo (or skip) and "Your link is ready" with Copy and Share, then the Floor. Back and a reload keep the answers (never the password). The stage or label name is set later in Profile (the username shows until then). Email + password, emailed one-time code, or Continue with Google (Gmail/Workspace only, Google account pinned, password to approve first link; website only, hidden inside the iPhone and Android apps for App Review 4.8, owner 15 Nov: the app adds `FloorTestApp` to its user agent, `src/lib/in-app.ts`; there log-in says "Signed up with Google? Use Email me a sign-in code", since the emailed code reaches any account; a missing or spoofed marker just shows Google again). Problems are shown under the field, one at a time; the server's answer sits by the button. Optional two-factor (authenticator + recovery codes; turning it on needs the password and signs out other devices, with an email), sign-in alerts. First visit lands on the Floor with "Welcome, {first}." and the link first

Where: `/signup`, `/login`, `/login/code`, `/signup/google`, `/login/google` **Bot protection (13 Nov):** a throwaway inbox is refused ("Use a permanent email address."); sign-up is capped per connection (10 an hour, 20 a day) and site-wide (100 an hour), much tighter when the bot check couldn't check; a new account's link page says "isn't open yet" and takes no uploads until its email is confirmed, and a link name left unconfirmed for 7 days is freed (SECURITY.md).

## Terms acceptance (15 Nov, Research brief)

The sign-up tick (guided and Google sign-up) reads "I'm 18 or over, and I have read, understood and agree to the Terms, including that music I send may be played by the receiver, and have read the Privacy Policy", with Terms and Privacy Policy linked. Ticking it records the Terms version, the time (UTC) and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the exact text, and emails a PDF copy stamped "Accepted by {name}, {email}, {date and time UTC}, Terms version {date}". When the Terms text changes, the next visit to the desk opens **Accept the Terms** ("The Terms have changed" if they accepted an older text): one tick, **Accept and continue**, then the Floor.

Nobody uses the desk or the phone app without agreeing to the current Terms (owner, 15 Nov): every desk page, action and API call, and every app request, waits for the tick; when the Terms can't be loaded, nobody gets in until they can. Someone who doesn't want to agree can still ask for a copy of their data or delete their account from the Terms page. Admins tick on their own desk; the ops console is not gated.

Where: `/signup`, `/signup/google`, `/accept-terms`, `src/lib/terms-record.ts`, `src/lib/auth.ts`, `src/lib/app-auth.ts` (`appUserAgreed`), `/api/v1/terms`

- **Pull to refresh (owner's rule, 31 Oct):** every page (the desk, the public pages, sign in and sign up, message pages, ops) refreshes with a deliberate pull from the top. Phone: rest at the top for half a second, drag down (it's heavy: about a quarter screen of travel) and let go; pulling back up before letting go, swiping sideways, or holding still never refreshes. Mouse: press on empty page (not a control, words or a field), drag down, let go. Trackpad: pulling past the top only arms it; a "Refresh" button drops in for 4 seconds and only a click refreshes. A refresh reloads the page's data in place (what's playing and anything typed stay) and tells live lists like the Floor and the Review Queue to catch up. It never starts inside dialogs, sheets, the ⌘K panel, fields, the player, the batch bar, waveform scrubbing or the Review card swipe, and stays off on the sign-up password step and while a field holds typed, unsent words. Inside the iPhone app the app's own native pull to refresh does the job instead. Code: `src/lib/pull-refresh.ts` (the rules, unit-tested), `src/components/PullToRefresh.tsx` (mounted once in the root layout).
