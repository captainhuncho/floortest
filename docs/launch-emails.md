# Beta launch copy: drafts

All drafts follow [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md). Replace {braces}. Nothing here has been sent.

## 1. Personal beta invite (founder → DJ, by DM or email)

**Subject:** A demo link for {callout name}?

> Hey {first name},
>
> I'm building FloorTest, a receiving desk for unreleased music. You share one link, artists upload the original WAV or MP3 (no account), and you work through them with play → Keep / Not for me → next. Kept tracks export as a Rekordbox crate.
>
> Senders get told when you've listened, downloaded or replied, so the "did you hear my track?" DMs stop.
>
> It's free for up to 20 active tracks, and Pro is free during the beta. I'd love you on it. Your invite code: {code}. Sign up: {APP_URL}/signup
>
> If it's useful I'd love 15 minutes of feedback after your first callout.
>
> {Your name}

## 4. Social posts

**Launch (Instagram / X / Threads):**
> Every demo in one place. Ready to play.
> FloorTest is a receiving desk for unreleased music. One link, original files, and artists hear when you've listened.
> Private beta now. Invite codes for DJs running callouts: link in bio.

**Sender angle:**
> Sent a track and heard nothing?
> On FloorTest you get an email when it's listened to (30 seconds of real play, not a click), downloaded, or replied to. No account needed.

**Product clip (10–15 s screen recording of Space → K → next):**
> Play. Keep. Next.
> That's the whole review loop on FloorTest.

**Honesty angle:**
> "Listened" on FloorTest means at least 30 seconds of real playback. Skipping around doesn't count. A signal should mean something.

**Callout template for beta DJs to post:**
> Taking {genre} for {night / mix / label} until {date}.
> Upload the WAV or MP3 here: {link}
> No account needed. You'll hear when I've listened.
