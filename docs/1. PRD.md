# Product Requirements

Summary only. The source of truth is the linked doc; when they disagree, the code wins, then that doc, then this file.

Full product: [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [01-product.md](01-product.md).

## Problem
DJs, curators, labels and radio get unreleased music scattered across DMs and links. Senders shout into a void and fear leaks.

## Users
- **Individual receivers** (DJs, curators) and **teams** (labels, radio, crews on one desk), equal priority.
- **Senders** (producers, artists, label reps), the growth loop. No account needed to send.

## Goals and success
Receivers clear their queue faster and find what they play out; senders trust the receipt; teams review together without losing control.

## Scope
One permanent link per receiver; WAV/MP3 uploads; Queue with Keep / Not for me; collections per callout; Prepare for set (Rekordbox XML, crate export); contacts; teams; sender receipts (Delivered, Listened, Downloaded, Replied, Played out); iOS and Android apps (Capacitor). Feature list screen by screen: [01-product.md](01-product.md).

## Constraints
Honest signals only ("Listened" = 30 s of real playback). Private beta, invite only, site behind a coming-soon password.
