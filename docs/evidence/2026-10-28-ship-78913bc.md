## What went live

- Hold and scan: an upload finished while the virus scanner sleeps or is down is held, not refused. The sender sees
  "received, being checked". `/api/cron/scan-held` scans the held batch every 10 minutes within the run's time limit,
  delivers clean tracks, and emails the sender if a file is flagged, refused, or still held after 24 hours. Stems sent
  from a receipt are held the same way.
- Batch C privacy: a team role change or invite cancel that changes nothing is refused and shown on the page; deletion
  takes everything; backups are one moment; decisions can't overwrite.
- Floor in the rule 15 style; system sans site-wide.
