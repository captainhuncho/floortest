## What shipped
- A personal invite opens an account only with the code from its email. Wrong guesses: 5 an hour per email and 20 per
  connection. New invite codes are 10 characters (older ones are 6; re-sending an invite from Ops gives it a new code).
- An account whose email isn't confirmed can't turn on two-factor or change its email; a password reset on an
  unconfirmed account clears whatever its creator set up.
- The e2e seed marks `demo@floortest.test` as email-confirmed, since two-factor now needs it.
- No migrations.
