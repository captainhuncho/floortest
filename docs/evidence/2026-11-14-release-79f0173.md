## What shipped
- When the bot check can't run (switched off, Cloudflare down, misconfigured), sign-up still works but is held to 2 a
  day per connection and 50 a day in all, and an incident is raised (at most one an hour). A failed check still
  refuses.
- Until a new account confirms its email, its link page says "…'s link isn't open yet" and every upload to it is
  refused. A link name left unconfirmed for 7 days is freed by the nightly clean-up. **Existing unconfirmed accounts
  stop receiving tracks until they confirm.**
- Throwaway inbox domains are refused: "Use a permanent email address."
- Sign-up is capped at 20 a day per connection and 100 an hour site-wide; both are shown on Admin → Security.
- Known limits (Backend, accepted by Safety): the site-wide cap counts every attempt, so a bot on many connections can
  use up the hour ("Sign-up is busy right now"); a freed account picks a new link name if it confirms later.
