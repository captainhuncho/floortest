## What shipped
- After a rename, the old link name forwards for 14 days (new `handle_history.forward_until`) and stays reserved for
  that account for 365 days (`expires_at`). A free return to your own held name keeps its original deadline.
- Super admins can recover a held name, with a required reason that is logged. A take-back ends every name the account held.
- Migration 0139 adds `forward_until`, copies the current `expires_at` into it, and extends names still held to 365 days
  from when they were created.
- 7 files against `main`, 240 lines added and 24 removed, plus this report.

## Known limits, from Safety
- Deploy window: a rename made while the build runs gets `forward_until` = 0, so that link will not forward. Low risk.
- A take-back onto the account's own still-held old name does not count against the yearly limit
  (`src/lib/accounts.ts:101`). Unlikely, because staff choose the target name.
- Open for the owner, legal wording: the privacy page may want a line that old link names are kept 365 days after a rename.
