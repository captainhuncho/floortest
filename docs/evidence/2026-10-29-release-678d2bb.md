## What shipped
- **App shell** (`mobile/`): the release Capacitor config allows only `floortest.app` (the `*.floortest.app` wildcard,
  which let any subdomain page read app-private files on Android, is gone); Android serves `/_capacitor_file_` only from
  the offline folder; offline downloads come only from FloorTest's own `/api/offline/file/` links, capped at 250 MB; the
  iOS share extension skips files over 250 MB; FileProvider paths narrowed; Android backup and device transfer off.
  Local dev sync now needs `FLOORTEST_DEV=1`.
- **Website** (`src/components/NativeApp.tsx`, `src/lib/native-share.ts`): signing out in the app empties the share
  inbox.
- `tests/mobile-shell.test.ts` (kept out of the Vercel build by `.vercelignore`).
- No migrations.
