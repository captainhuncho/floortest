## What shipped
- Held for 8 s with Undo, through #353's signed token and single-transaction cancel and revert:
  - personal invites (Undo revokes the codes not yet sent);
  - verifying from a request, from a proposal, and directly (Undo removes the mark and `identity_verified_at`, puts the
    note back to the account's own earlier note, sets an approved proposal back to pending with the same proposer, and
    sets a direct verification's own record to withdrawn while reopening the colleague proposals it had declined);
  - rejecting and removing a verification (Undo restores the earlier state, status-guarded).
- A 0.5 s grace on the sender's own delivery (8.5 s), so a last-moment Undo isn't raced by the same process; the Undo
  window itself still closes at about 8 s.
- Four eyes holds through Undo: only the approver can undo their approval, and afterwards the proposer still can't
  approve their own proposal. Undo never adds a Verified mark.
- Ops only.
