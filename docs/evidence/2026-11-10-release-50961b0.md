## What shipped
- The owner of a desk with an open callout can share it as a PNG card, story (1080x1920) or square (1080x1080), with
  the desk's link and a QR code. Route `GET /api/callout-card/[id]?format=story|square`: signed-in owner with a verified
  email only; other people, bad ids and staff view-as get the same 404; a closed, full, paused or unavailable callout
  gets 409; rate limited (429).
- Names in any script are drawn with HarfBuzz and Noto fonts. Arabic or Hebrew mixed with Latin, and some rare
  characters, show the handle instead of the name.
