## What shipped
- **Invite link past the gate** (#81): `/invite/CODE` (and `/signup?invite=CODE`, which redirects there) sets the
  coming-soon pass and opens sign-up, but only for a live personal invite: one with an email, not revoked, not used up,
  and whose email has no account yet. Shared Ops codes, `BETA_INVITE_CODES`, referral codes and unknown codes go back to
  `/coming-soon`. 20 tries per address per 10 minutes (`src/app/invite/[code]/route.ts`, `liveInviteCode` in
  `src/lib/invites.ts`, `/invite` on `GATE_OPEN` in `src/proxy.ts`); the query is bound, the redirect stays on the
  app's own origin. Trade-off: whoever opens a live link before the invitee signs up gets the same gate pass as the site
  password. `docs/03-app-flow.md` and `docs/prebeta-run.md` updated; test in `tests/invites.test.ts`.
- **Plan** (#80): `docs/06-plan.md` marks passkeys shipped (release #78).
- No migrations.
