## What shipped
- Personal beta invites: used once, 14 days. `invites.used_at` is set inside the sign-up transaction (a second racing
  sign-up rolls back); deleting the account no longer revives the invite; signing up any other way marks every invite
  waiting for that email. Resend and Restore give a fresh 14 days; a used invite can't be resent or restored.
- A used or expired invite link shows "This invite has been used or has expired" with Log in (`/invite/used`); a dead
  team invite goes to `/join/<invite>`. Team invite accept is a guarded update (not accepted, not revoked, not expired).
- Email confirmation links last 48 hours. Changing or resetting a password, turning two-step on, or changing email also
  ends emailed sign-in codes, two-step tickets and a pending email change.
