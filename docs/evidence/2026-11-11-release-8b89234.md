## What shipped
- Invite codes and newsletter confirm tokens are kept only as hashes; every lookup goes by the hash. New random codes
  are FLOOR- plus 20 characters (100 bits); Ops sees codes by their last 4 characters.
- Ops → Invites shows a new shared code once, from the page's memory (never a cookie), with Copy, until Done or leaving
  the page. Resend sends a new code. Newsletter unsubscribe links are signed per subscriber; links already emailed keep
  working. Backups leave out the hashes.
