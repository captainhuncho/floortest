- Review Queue in the rule 15 style: the list as one flat panel, the open track as a card, Keep the one lime button (`cc7c416`).
- Queue answers taps at once: a tapped or next track shows while the server answers; the buttons come with the page; one order at every width (`8a9ead8`).
- Back and forward through the Queue on phones: ‹ › and a sideways swipe move between tracks; swipe no longer decides (`b7be657`).
- Collections as folders with status tabs; the receiving callout's newest tracks under them; a collection's page reads its numbers on one line; shared list rows with square play buttons and plain-text status (`80e86e2`).
- Receipt: the rule 15 restyle was built and reverted on the owner's word; the receipt keeps its earlier layout in the system sans (`22155f4`, `f4c193b`, `e086e64`).
- Send page in one column at every width, the receiver's photo, name and callout centred at the top and kept there once a file is picked (`33d38f2`, replacing `f58b5b6` and `28870f9`).
- Send page profile bar: a slim bar with photo, name and callout comes down once the profile scrolls away (`d90520a`).
- No migrations, no API, permission or data changes.
