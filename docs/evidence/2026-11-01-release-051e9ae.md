## What shipped
- `src/lib/sessions.ts`: `readSessionToken` reads only the current cookie (`__Host-floortest_session` on the live
  site). The fallback that still read the old plain `floortest_session` until 15 Nov is removed: any
  `*.floortest.app` subdomain could plant that name, the `__Host-` one it cannot.
- `src/lib/auth.ts`: signing in also clears the old cookie from the browser.
- Effect: a browser that still holds only the old cookie (signed in about 1–15 Oct and not since) is signed out once
  and signs in again. Nobody else notices anything.
- `tests/legacy-session-cookie.test.ts` (new), `tests/session-limits.test.ts`; `SECURITY.md` and `docs/06-plan.md`
  updated.
- `docs/legal-review.md` still names the old cookie; that is the owner's legal wording and is left as it is.
