## What shipped
- **#89** team member activation; migrations `0106_feedback_actor` (feedback from a team desk is signed by the teammate)
  and `0107_preview_passes_used` (a draft-preview pass works once).
- **#92** per-person database rules on columns: `0108_person_columns` (as `ft_person`, `users` can be read and changed
  only through the listed columns, so a teammate's password hash, two-step secret, recovery codes or Google id can't be
  read) and `0109_person_suspended_desks` (a suspended team desk is shut to its teammates). Tested on PGlite and in e2e;
  first run on Neon is this deploy.
- **#87** uploaded audio is checked to be audio; uploads still in progress count toward desk capacity; release ids
  scoped to the desk; one email normalisation everywhere.
- **#88** bulk quarantine; the crate export goes through `crateTracks` and leaves held files out.
- **#96** the invite link's gate pass lasts one day (was 30); only codes of 16+ characters (`FLOOR-` plus 10) open the
  gate, and Ops Resend swaps an old code for a long one; tests for `/invite/[code]` and the proxy redirect.
