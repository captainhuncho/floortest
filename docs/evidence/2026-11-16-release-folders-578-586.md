## What shipped
- Callouts and Projects each get their own folders. A folder holds one kind only, one level, private to the desk, with
  no folder cap (the tier's track allowance is the only limit).
- One "+ New" menu: in Callouts, New callout and New folder; in Projects, Upload audio, New project and New folder. The
  same menu opens on right-click of empty space, Shift+F10 or the menu key. A card's right-click opens its ··· menu.
- Folder cards show a 2×2 of the first covers, with Open, Rename and Delete folder. "Move to folder" is in a callout's
  or project's ··· menu. Deleting a folder leaves its callouts and projects in place, in no folder.
- Covers without art show the name on a plain well.
- Projects loses the explainer text and the New project tile; Callouts loses the New collection tile.
- Server: batch "Move to project" for up to 100 picked tracks, with each track checked for ownership on its own.
