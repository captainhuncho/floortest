## Conventions
Access classes (public, authenticated receiver, shared access, capability links, admin, service callbacks), validation, errors, audio responses: [API_GUIDE.md](API_GUIDE.md).

## App API
`/api/v1` for the mobile app: `auth/login`, `auth/refresh`, `auth/logout`, `me`, `queue`, `tracks/:id/decision`, `tracks/:id/audio`, `receivers/:handle`, `sends`, `sends/complete` (200 `{ token }`, or 202 `{ held: true, message }` while the virus scanner sleeps: the track arrives within about 15 minutes), `collections`, `prepare`.

## Webhooks
Resend delivery webhook `/api/webhooks/resend`.

## Rate limits
Vercel Firewall: 300 requests a minute per address on `/api/*`, 60 a minute on sign-in pages; per-action caps in code.

## Callout share card

`GET /api/callout-card/[id]?format=story|square`: a PNG (1080x1920 or 1080x1080) of an open callout, for the account's owner only. Team members, support "view as" and other accounts get 404. 401 signed out, 403 email not confirmed, 400 unknown format, 429 with `Retry-After` (20 a minute per person), 409 `{ reason: "closed" | "full" | "paused" | "unavailable" }` when the callout can't be shared. A suspended account or a callout that isn't active gets "unavailable". ("invite-only" was retired on 11 Nov: cards work while the site is behind the Coming soon password, since receiving links open without it.) Photo order: collection artwork, then the receiver's cover, then the blank card; a photo under 438px on its shorter side is skipped. Printed fields come only from what the public link page shows (`src/lib/callout-card/fields.ts`). The open rule is `calloutBlock` in `src/lib/desk.ts`, shared with the public page. Text is shaped from committed fonts: the card's own, then Noto for Latin extended, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. Emoji are dropped; a name that can't be drawn prints the handle, and a callout or details line that can't be drawn is left out. Right-to-left text mixed with Latin letters, or with numbers that aren't whole words, counts as can't be drawn, because its order could print wrong; the BPM still prints.

`POST /api/callout-card/[id]` with JSON `{ format: "story" | "square", note?: string }`: the same card with the owner's own line under the callout title (13 Nov). Same checks and answers as GET, plus 400 `{ reason: "note-too-long" }` over 80 characters (counted as people count them, after emoji and control characters are taken out), 400 for a body that isn't JSON or an unknown format, and 413 for a body over 4096 characters (or a `Content-Length` over 16384 bytes, refused before the body is read). Another account's callout id gets the same 404 as GET. The note travels in the body, never the address, and is not stored. It prints in lime serif italic, up to 2 lines on the story card and 1 on the square; if the square card can't fit both, the note stays and the genre/BPM line is left out. A note that can't be drawn is left out. Uses `currentDeskForChange()` like other POST routes.
