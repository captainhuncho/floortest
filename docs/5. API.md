## Conventions

## App API
`/api/v1` for the mobile app: `auth/login`, `auth/refresh`, `auth/logout`, `me`, `queue`, `tracks/:id/decision`, `tracks/:id/audio`, `receivers/:handle`, `sends`, `sends/complete` (200 `{ token }`, or 202 `{ held: true, message }` while the virus scanner sleeps: the track arrives within about 15 minutes), `collections`, `prepare`.

## Webhooks
Resend delivery webhook `/api/webhooks/resend`.

## Rate limits
Vercel Firewall: 300 requests a minute per address on `/api/*`, 60 a minute on sign-in pages; per-action caps in code.

## Callout share card

`GET /api/callout-card/[id]?format=story|square`: a PNG (1080x1920 or 1080x1080) of an open callout, for the account's owner only. Team members, support "view as" and other accounts get 404. 401 signed out, 403 email not confirmed, 400 unknown format, 429 with `Retry-After` (20 a minute per person), 409 `{ reason: "closed" | "full" | "paused" | "unavailable" | "invite-only" }` when the callout can't be shared. "invite-only" means the site is behind the Coming soon password: cards stay off there because the QR would land strangers on the password page; a suspended account or a callout that isn't active still gets "unavailable". Photo order: collection artwork, then the receiver's cover, then the blank card; a photo under 438px on its shorter side is skipped. Printed fields come only from what the public link page shows (`src/lib/callout-card/fields.ts`). The open rule is `calloutBlock` in `src/lib/desk.ts`, shared with the public page. Text is shaped from committed fonts: the card's own, then Noto for Latin extended, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil, Thai, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. Emoji are dropped; a name that can't be drawn prints the handle, and a callout or details line that can't be drawn is left out. Right-to-left text mixed with Latin letters, or with numbers that aren't whole words, counts as can't be drawn, because its order could print wrong; the BPM still prints.
