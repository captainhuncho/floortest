Desk audit: what to fix before strangers send music

Five reviewers went through every main screen at phone and laptop width: visual design, accessibility, UX flows, copy and motion. The 7-voice panel then sorted it into four batches. Nothing has been changed yet.

Every colour token passes contrast in Night and Day. Focus rings, dialogs and the swipe's button fallback all hold. The design is sound; the drift is in the details.

The pattern across all five

  • Lime has spread. Review shows about 8 lime marks at once, and the play button is lime next to Keep. Home uses lime as text colour.
  • One thing, several names and shapes. "The Floor / Review Queue / Prepare for set" on laptop vs "Floor / Queue / Prepare" on phone. Three track-row designs. Six "selected" styles.
  • The phone review loop isn't finished. The rail is mid-screen and scrolls away, the swipe snaps the old track back while saving, buttons dim to 40%, and clearing the queue ends on nothing.
  • Senders can lose a send. The form lives only in memory while they fetch the email code; errors don't say whether anything was sent.
  • Keep: the public send page (the bar for everything else), the home set pieces, the decision rail, the tactile sidebar, the Prepare rows, the mono type.

The four batches

1 · One light, one name

Panel pick · before 17 Oct

About 25 small fixes · 1–1.5 days · no layout changes

  • Lime only on Keep and the one call to action: play buttons, tab underlines, selected rows, chips and home labels go bone or neutral.
  • One set of names: Floor · Queue · Collections · Prepare on both navs. "Submissions" becomes "Link open / Link paused".
  • "Clear kept" stops looking like a delete: ghost button, "Start a new batch", no red.
  • "Desk full" banner gives the limit and points to Prepare, not only to plans.
  • Home: one action label while invite-only ("Request invite"); the hero box says "Check it's free".
  • Senders: answers survive leaving for the email code, a leave warning during upload, "Keep this page open", errors that say "Nothing was sent".
  • Receipt gets "Send another to Maxwell". Played out email loses the emoji and explains the signal.
  • Access: Not for me rows readable, seek bar out of the link, upload screen stops reading every byte, scroll padding under fixed bars, tap targets to 44px, Undo toast pauses on touch.
  • Confirm-email banner only on Floor and Send.

2 · Review on the phone, finished

week of 19 Oct

About 2 days · e2e on review · feel-check on a real phone via the app build

  • Not for me · Keep · Reply docked at the bottom in thumb reach; tab bar hides while reviewing; Download moves into Details.
  • Swipe v2: decided track leaves and stays gone, the next slides in, a quick flick counts, buttons no longer dim.
  • The Keep stamp lands at the line with the haptic.
  • Clearing the queue ends on "All caught up. 2 kept ready" with Prepare one tap away.
  • Focus moves to the next track's title after a decision; the sender's message sits above the rail.
  • Queue list and title first on phone; tabs in one scrolling row.

3 · One system

after beta starts

About 4–5 days · the bigger layout work

  • One track row for Queue, Prepare, collections and Floor, built from the Prepare rows.
  • Collections as a crate list, one row per callout; no gradient tiles or stat tiles.
  • Floor as a desk: numbers in one readout strip; laptop gets the "two sides of the desk" two-column layout.
  • One selected style, one tabs style, one page width, one title size; shared form, card and button styles fixed at the source.
  • Activity laid out like the home page set log; Contacts as tight two-line rows with neutral avatars.
  • A visible field-border token; Day-mode waveform contrast; keyboard shortcuts switch in Settings.

4 · Small moments

any time

About half a day · restrained

  • A one-time ring on the receipt's dot when "Listened" arrives.
  • The send bar holds at 100%, then a tick draws itself.
  • Player shows a buffering ring instead of Pause over silence on big WAVs.
  • Upload bars move smoothly instead of jumping.

Panel round

DJ / A&R
Batch 2 is what I'd feel most: the rail in thumb reach and a swipe that doesn't hang. Batch 1's lime fix means Keep is the only bright thing again. Wants Played out on any day, not only Mon/Tue.
Label owner
One name per screen matters when teammates talk to each other ("check the Queue"). Fine with Collections as a list; the tiles read as decoration on a team desk.
Sender / producer
Losing a 250 MB send because I went to get a code is the worst thing in the report. "Nothing was sent" in errors and "Send another" on the receipt both earn a place in batch 1.
Designer
Agrees batch 1 first: it's cheap and makes batch 3 easier. Holds the line on no tile grids; the set log and crate list are the targets.
Engineer
Batch 1 is safe for the 17th. Batch 2 changes review state, so e2e runs and a real-phone check before it ships; not the day before beta widens.
Ops & support
"Clear kept" reading as delete and vague upload errors are support tickets waiting to happen. Both in batch 1.
Privacy & legal
Objects to starting the upload before Send: that holds a file before consent; needs the lawyer and a retention rule, so it's out of all batches. The link-page line about sharing changes only with the lawyer's wording.

Changed because of the panel: the early-upload idea is parked; sender safety and "Clear kept" moved up into batch 1; batch 2 waits for a real-phone check.

Your calls

  1. Go: build batch 1 now on mobile-next for a preview before 17 Oct, then batch 2 the week of 19 Oct? Panel recommends yes.
  2. Played out on any day? Add "Played it out" to each Prepare card and keep the Mon/Tue prompt as a nudge. DJ / A&R: yes.
  3. Required profile on the send form: keep it as an anti-impersonation rule (then it goes in the docs), or make it optional?
  4. "Up next" on the Floor lists the newest while the queue opens the oldest. Rename it "Just in", or list the oldest first?
  5. "Clear kept" → "Start a new batch"? Or another name you'd use in the booth.
  6. Phone tab label "Prepare" is cut to "PREPAR" at 390px. Shorten to "Prep", or tighten the label?
  7. Keyboard shortcuts (K, X, 1–4) are always on, an access fail. Add an on/off switch in Settings (on by default)?
