# Rollout plan: DJ-led launch, London and Lagos

**Shape:** DJs and curators come first. Every track sent to them brings in a producer (the growth loop). Labels, radio shows and crews come later, as the upgrade ("bring your crew").
**Two numbers we watch:** (a) the north star, the share of tracks decided within 7 days; (b) the onboarding number, the share of new desks that receive their first track within 48 hours of sign-up.
**Honest-signals rule:** no stats, quotes, logos or press until they're real. Placeholders stay placeholders.

---

## 1. Phases (L = public launch day)

| # | When | What happens | Who we target | One metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **L-6 to L-4** | Founding-DJ programme opens by hand: 30 DJs on the list, 10 to 15 onboarded, one call each, first callout posted with the template. Story kit drafted. | DJs in London and Lagos who already run weekly callouts (Afro house, amapiano, house) | First track within 48 h: 80% of founding desks |
| 2 | **L-4 to L-2** | Founding DJs run live callouts. We sit in on their first review session. The receipt line goes live for senders without an account ("DJ too? Get your own link", waitlist while invite-only). Talks start with 2 radio shows. | Founding DJs, plus the producers who send to them | Tracks decided within 7 days: 60% or more on founding desks |
| 3 | **L-2 to L** | Waitlist opens in waves through invite codes (5 per founding DJ). Launch nights are booked. Callout of the week starts on Instagram. Beta-pricing email goes out (6 months free, then 50% off for a year). | Waitlist, producers who received a receipt, the founding DJs' peers | First track within 48 h: 70% of new desks |
| 4 | **L to L+2** | Launch week: the London night and the Lagos night, radio segments go live, a newsletter feature, sign-up opens. | Club and online-radio DJs, curators, producer communities | First track within 48 h: 60% of new desks (the wider crowd is colder) |
| 5 | **L+2 to L+5** | Sender loop push. Receipt sign-ups tracked. Producer communities invited to send to live callouts. Weekly callout of the week. | Producers who DJ, plus Discord and WhatsApp producer groups | Decided within 7 days: holds at 50% or more as volume grows |
| 6 | **L+5 to L+8** | Team upgrade story ("bring your crew"). Approaches to labels, radio teams and crews that already use a member's desk. First real partner quotes published, with permission. | Small labels, radio shows, DJ crews and collectives | Decided within 7 days on team desks vs solo desks (team desks should match or beat solo) |

## 2. Ten rollout ideas

| # | Name | What | Why it works here | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Founding-DJ programme** | 30 hand-picked DJs (15 London, 15 Lagos and Accra) get early access, a direct line to the owner and their name on a founding wall, with their consent. | DJs trust other DJs; a known name's link in a bio beats any ad. | Low |
| 2 | **Callout of the week** | Every Friday we feature one live callout with its brief (genre, BPM, deadline) and the DJ's link. | Producers get a real reason to send, and the DJ gets tracks within 48 h. Both of our numbers move at once. | Low |
| 3 | **Link-in-bio and QR story kit** | A ready caption, a QR story card and a 10-second clip showing Keep and then the next track, all in brand style. | Onboarding's one job is the first track in 48 h, and a story is where producers see it. | Low |
| 4 | **Radio-show partnerships** | One London and one Lagos online radio show take demos only through their FloorTest link. Once a month, the host plays the kept tracks live on air. | Shows are already teams (host, producer, booker): the team story told on air. | Med |
| 5 | **Receipt sender loop** | The receipt shows "DJ too? Get your own link" after delivery. We measure how many senders sign up and tune the wording, never the volume. | Many producers DJ, and they've just seen it work for them. | Low |
| 6 | **City launch nights** | A night in London and one in Lagos. Founding DJs play tracks they kept on FloorTest, and their producers are invited. QR codes on the tables, no stage pitch. | Makes "from the studio to the floor" visible in a real room. | High |
| 7 | **Creator newsletters** | Short features or sponsored mentions in DJ-tool and Afro house/amapiano newsletters, built around one founding DJ's workflow. | Readers are the people sent demos every week. | Med |
| 8 | **Invite codes, DJ to DJ** | Each active desk gets 5 codes, worded like passing on a tip rather than a referral scheme. Codes are released when the desk has made 10 decisions. | Growth goes to desks that actually use FloorTest, so the north star isn't diluted. | Low |
| 9 | **Producer-community callouts** | Founding DJs post callouts straight into producer Discords and forums, and we answer questions in the thread. | Producers arrive with the brief read, so tracks fit and decisions come faster. | Low |
| 10 | **Lagos WhatsApp callout drops** | Founding Lagos DJs share their callout card and link in their WhatsApp broadcast lists and Status. The kit includes a WhatsApp-sized card and a one-line text version. | In Lagos and Accra demos move on WhatsApp; meet producers there. | Low |
| 11 | **Detty December crate** (Lagos and Accra) | At L+6 to L+8, founding DJs run "December crate" callouts ahead of the festive season, with prep shown in Prepare and the Rekordbox export. | The busiest booking season in West Africa, when DJs need fresh records most. | Med |
| 12 | **Lagos producer sessions** | Small listening sessions with a producer collective or studio: tracks are sent live to a DJ's link, decided in the room, and the receipt shows up on the producer's phone. | Shows the honest receipt in person, where leaks and ghosting are the fears. | Med |

## 3. Channels, ranked

1. **Instagram:** callout of the week cards, founding-DJ link-in-bio stories, 10-second Keep clips, launch-night reels.
2. **WhatsApp (groups, Status, broadcast lists):** callout cards and links from DJs themselves; plain one-liners, never bulk sends from us.
3. **In-person events:** launch nights and producer sessions, with QR codes on the tables and kept tracks played out loud.
4. **Radio:** monthly segments where the host plays kept tracks, plus a credit line saying the show's link is on FloorTest.
5. **Discord and producer communities:** live callouts with briefs, owner Q&A on how receipts work (what "Listened" means: 30 seconds of real playback).
6. **TikTok:** screen-recorded clips of a review session (play, Keep, next) and a "what happens to your track" explainer from the sender's side.
7. **Music press and newsletters:** one founding DJ's workflow story at launch; real numbers only after L+4.

## 5. What not to do

- **No paid boosts or "pay to be heard" for senders,** ever. Senders are free. The queue is oldest first and nobody buys their way up.
- **No fake scarcity or invented proof.** No made-up waitlist counts, user numbers, quotes, logos or "as heard on" lines. No "verified artist".
- **No mass messaging on DJs' behalf.** We don't bulk-message WhatsApp groups or scrape producers, and we never share a receiver's decisions publicly. Keep and Not for me stay private.
