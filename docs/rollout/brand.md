# Brand and positioning (12 Oct 2026)

## 1. Positioning
1. **One line:** untitled.stream is where the maker keeps their music; FloorTest is where that music lands, gets heard and decided on, and the sender is told honestly what happened.
2. **Against file links, DMs, WeTransfer, Dropbox, promo pools:** those deliver a file and go quiet. FloorTest is a desk: one link that never changes, a queue, Keep / Not for me, a crate for the set, and a receipt for the sender.
3. **What we own:** the receiving side, and honest signals ("Listened" means 30 seconds of real playback, and we say so).

## 2. Lines
**Taglines / category lines**
1. *The receiving desk for unreleased music.* Already the category line; keep it. **Favourite.**
2. *One link. Every track.* Says the mechanic in four words.
3. *Play, keep, next.* The rhythm of the queue; good for clips and stickers.
4. *Know what happened next.* The sender's side, for receipts and sender emails.
5. *Where unreleased music lands.* Plain contrast with maker-side tools.

**Home headlines**
1. *From private tracks / to the DJ set.* The current promise; the serif italic on line two does the work. **Favourite**: keep it.
2. *Every track in one place. / Ready to play.* Already approved in the voice guide; more literal.
3. *Stop digging through DMs.* Names the pain; use as a second line or ad, not the hero (it leads with a negative).

## 3. Visual signature
1. **The Booth at 2am:** near-black scenes, warm and quiet; real booths, bedrooms and night buses in London and Lagos. No bright daylight hero shots.
2. **One light:** in every image exactly one lime point (a cue button, a phone's Keep, a live dot). If two things glow, crop one out.
3. **The crest** sits small and still, like a mark on gear, never animated or used as a pattern.
4. **Mono type as readouts:** BPM, 30 s, 250 MB, 90 days set in JetBrains Mono over imagery like panel markings; the serif italic only on the home headline.
5. **Motion is the product doing one thing:** a 10-second clip of Keep → next track, or a receipt line turning bright when Listened lands. Fades and small scale-ins only; no flashes, no beat-synced cuts.

## 5. PR
**Story angles** (facts only from the repo)
1. **The other side of the demo:** music tools serve makers; FloorTest serves the DJ or label receiving new tracks every week.
2. **A receipt that doesn't lie:** "Listened" means 30 seconds of real playback; the DJ's Keep / Not for me stays private. Angle for producer newsletters.
3. **Built for London and Lagos:** launching in the UK, EU, Ghana and Nigeria at the same price; solo DJs and curators in both scenes lead. Operator being registered in Ghana.
4. **Your files stay yours:** originals never transcoded, virus scanning on our own servers (no third-party scanner), unkept files deleted after 90 days.
5. **Senders never pay:** no paid boosts, no pay-to-be-heard; the free link is the business case for honesty. Contrast with paid submission platforms.

**Launch announcement (draft)**
> FloorTest is open: the receiving desk for unreleased music.
> DJs, curators and labels get one link for every track that's sent to them, a queue to play, keep or pass, and a crate ready for the set.
> Senders get a private receipt that says what actually happened. Free for your first 20 tracks.

## 6. Never say or show
1. **A signal or claim bigger than the truth:** no "verified artist", no "heard by" for a 5-second skip, no invented user counts, quotes, logos or press. Use "{first partner quote}" until one exists.
2. **That a sender is being rejected or ignored:** no "passed", no mocking demos, no "never answer another DM" jokes at artists' expense.
3. **Hype and leak imagery:** no exclamation marks, "game-changer", party crowds with hands up, or anything that suggests tracks are public, shared or streamable by others.
